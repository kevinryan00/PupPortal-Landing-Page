FAQ

Questions daycare owners are likely to ask.

What does PupPortal actually replace?

It brings capacity-led bookings, dog records, vaccination status and quick daily owner updates into one place, reducing reliance on scattered messages and paper records.

Can owners see other dogs or customers?

No. The customer experience is designed around a private Pupdate showing information for their own dog.

How quickly can staff add an update?

The core care updates are designed as one-tap actions: Ate, Walked, Played and Napped.

What happens if a vaccination is expired?

The product is designed to clearly flag vaccination status and prevent an expired dog from being booked or checked in.

Does PupPortal include payments or accounting?

No. Accounting functionality, a payment portal and schedule reminders are deliberately outside the initial product scope.

Is there a set-up cost?

Yes. The proposed model combines a one-off set-up fee with a monthly subscription. The exact set-up fee can be agreed based on the configuration required.
