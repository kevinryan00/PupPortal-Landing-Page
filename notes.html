CASE H:

Task 1:

Scheduling software for a kennel/dog minding business

40 places / 6 staff

Vaccination records easily available

Hunch: Any business dealing with pets that need to schedule appointments whether that’s full days for kennel/day care services or shorter appointment times for grooming/veterinary purposes would pay for this to keep their operations running smoothly from a scheduling and safety viewpoint.

A strong niche within this market is small independent dog daycare centres that manage bookings manually and have limited daily capacity.

The daycare centre buys, both the company employees and customers use it as a booking and communication system. Alerts are in place to ensure the wellbeing of the dogs.

Invented, daycare owner:_"I'm turning dogs away on busy days while I've empty spaces on other days. I need one place to control bookings and make sure every dog's vaccinations are up to date."_

Invented, daycare staff member:_"I don't have time to answer messages all day or write detailed updates. I need to tap once when a dog has eaten, walked, played or napped and get back to looking after the dogs."_

It is an online booking system that can take bookings through a portal and provide owners with regular updates on how their dogs are progressing throughout the day.

Two similar existing products below. Both links checked 19/10/2026.

PetExec \- https://www.petexec.net/

Gingr \- https://www.gingrapp.com/

=================================================================

Assumption - Select typical SAAS model pricing with implementation fee followed by monthly instalments. This allows for small upfront payment to setup the software in a bespoke fashion for the buyer (covering professional service fees for the software consultant) followed by monthly payments to cover ongoing support and hosting.

PRD

  • Hypothesis: Provide easy to use software to schedule dogs, provide customers with updates and stay compliant from a vaccination standpoint.
  • Audience: Dog daycare owners and managers.
  • Call to Action: “Free up your time to focus on what matters”, “Enquire Now”
  • Success: Stable sales enquiries from prospective clients and minimum support calls from existing customers.
  • Out of Scope: Accounting functionality, payment portal, schedule reminders.

Alpha Test

Tested a booking for a dog. Didn’t like that there was initially no search field for the dog, only a dropdown so I changed that. I then booked another and it left the dogs name in the search field after I booked the dog in so asked the AI builder to clear out the dog’s name after I booked it in. There was no way to add a new dog outside of the sample data so changed that. There was no way to view a dogs schedule history so added account functionality so a user could look up visit history and vaccine expiry.

The main constraint of adding notes was tested with one click. I didn’t like that you couldn’t undo this on an earlier version so I changed it allowing you to unclick a note in case of user error.
